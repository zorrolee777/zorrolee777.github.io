About me

I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at Stanford University. My current research focuses on CMOS oscillator-based computing architectures for ultra-energy-efficient deep learning, computer vision, and optimization. My prior work spans RISC-V systems, advanced-node digital design, and radiation-hardened integrated circuits.
